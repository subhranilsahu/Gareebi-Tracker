RESPONSIBILITY SPLIT & ACCESS RULES
==============================================================================
1. Abhigyan (Frontend Lead):
   - Scope: templates/*, static/*
   - Tools: HTML5, CSS3 (Modern Flexbox/Grid), JavaScript (ES6+ Vanilla)
   - Goal: Build responsive, accessible, dark-themed, sleek UI.

2. Subhranil (Backend Lead):
   - Scope: src/*, data/*, tests/*, requirements.txt

==============================================================================
PAGE-BY-PAGE ROADMAP
==============================================================================
1. Landing Page (landing.html): First impression, product value, links to login/signup.
2. Authentication (login.html & signup.html): Clean credential input, client validation.
3. Dashboard (dashboard.html): High-level wealth & spending cockpit.
4. Profile (profile.html): Account details with tasteful Gen-Z financial humor.
5. Transactions (transactions.html): Deep-dive audit ledger of all expenses & incomes.
